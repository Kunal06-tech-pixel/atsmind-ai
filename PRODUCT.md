# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

ATSmind serves job seekers improving a resume for a target role and recruiters evaluating consented candidate resumes against explicit job requirements.

## Product Purpose

ATSmind turns resumes and job requirements into inspectable, evidence-based decision support. Candidates can analyze, improve, discuss, build, and export resumes. Recruiters can define requirements, evaluate candidates, compare evidence, record notes, and manage shortlists.

## Positioning

Deterministic scoring remains separate from generative AI. Requirements, gaps, and supporting resume evidence stay inspectable; AI explains and suggests but does not calculate or modify the score.

## Operating Context

The product works with resume PDFs, job descriptions, role requirements, saved analyses, candidate evaluations, and recruiter shortlists through distinct Career and Recruit workspaces.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve React 19, JSX, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, Lucide React, and React Router.
- Preserve existing authentication, authorization, backend APIs, scoring, builder, recruiter, and admin behavior.
- The product is decision-support software, not a job board, scraper, marketplace, or automated hiring system.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is ATSmind AI. Preserve the existing document-analysis mark and the teal/mint identity. The public landing page follows the user-supplied Aura cinematic reference while translating every product surface into resume intelligence.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains real Career, Recruit, builder, comparison, authorization, and analysis flows. No approved customer names, testimonials, usage metrics, prices, accuracy figures, or hiring outcomes are available and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make evidence inspectable.
- Keep deterministic scoring distinct from AI assistance.
- Keep Career and Recruit equally visible.
- Preserve human responsibility for employment decisions.
- Label demonstration data as sample content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public navigation, menus, tabs, toggles, and actions must be keyboard accessible, retain visible focus, support reduced motion, and work from 320px upward.
